Chapter 04

My Trust 🤍

Because love is not only what we say. It is what we protect, respect and choose every day.

🥹 Maa Amma

Naa amma evaritho kuda ekkuvaga matladadhu. Tanu evaritho aina matladali anipinche situation vachina, first naatho “Matladana? Vaddha?” ani aduguthundi. Naaku teliyakunda em cheyyadhu, prati vishayam naatho share cheskuntundi, em cheyyalanna nanney aduguthundi.

Tana loyalty, care, and concern ante naaku chaala istam. ❤️ Anduke tanu ante naaku antha istam. 🥺❤️

🌷 Past & Growth

Past lo jarigina konni vishayalu nannu hurt chesayi. Kaani nuvvu aa situation ni realise cheskoni, nee behaviour ni marchukunnav. Aa change ni, nee love ni, nee loyalty ni nenu chaala value chestha. ❤️

Anduke past ni pattukoni ninnu judge cheyyadam naaku ishtam ledu. Past lo em jarigindi anedi kanna, ippudu nuvvu naatho ela untunnav, entha genuine ga nannu premisthunnav anedhe naaku important.

🫂 The Trust In My Heart

Ippudu nee meedha naaku unna nammakam enti ante… nuvvu naa daggara emii daachavu ani, naaku teliyakunda emii cheyyavu ani naa heart completely nammutundi. 🥹❤️

And nanna… aa trust ni eppudu jagrathaga chusko. 🫂❤️
Endukante nenu ninnu just love cheyyadam kaadu… naa whole heart tho ninnu nammutunna.

Nee meedha naaku chacche antha nammakam, prema unnayi nanna. 🥹❤️

❤️‍🩹 One thing I need you to know

Incase nuvvu eppudaina malli ala chesthe, appudu nenu emi cheppalenu amma. Nenu godava cheyyanu, kopam tho maatladanu, ninnu blame cheyyanu… just silent ga undipotha.

Endukante appatiki nenu cheppalsina prati maata cheppesi untanu… nenu nammina person daggara nunchi malli ade pain vasthe, daanni words lo explain cheyyadaniki naa daggara strength undadu. ❤️‍🩹

I genuinely don't think I can take it anymore.

Nenu ninnu punish cheyyalani silent ga undanu nanna… naa heart ki inkem cheppalo teliyaka silent aipotha.

Kaani ippudu ivanni chepthunnadi bhayapettadaniki kaadu nanna… naa heart lo nee meedha unna nammakam, prema entha deep ga undo cheppadaniki. 🥹❤️

Naa trust ni, naa love ni nv eppdu jagrathaga chusko bangaram. 🫂❤️
